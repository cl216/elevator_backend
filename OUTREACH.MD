# Teacher Recruitment Plan (Pre‑Launch Supply Seeding)

Goal: Recruit **20--40 teachers in one city (start with Dublin)** before
launch so the map feels active with **40--100 sessions**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Daily 2‑Hour Routine

## 1. Find Potential Teachers (30 minutes)

Search these platforms and collect **\~20 new leads per day**.

Platforms: - Instagram - Eventbrite - Meetup - Google Maps

Search examples: - "Dublin guitar teacher" - "Dublin art classes" -
"Dublin cooking classes" - "Dublin photography workshop" - "Ireland
craft workshop"

Add leads to spreadsheet:

  Name      Skill    City     Contact     Status
  --------- -------- -------- ----------- --------
  Example   Guitar   Dublin   Instagram   Sent

Daily goal: **20 new leads**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Send Outreach Messages (20 minutes)

Send **15--20 messages per day**.

Example outreach message:

Hi --- I came across your \[guitar/painting/cooking\] classes and they
looked great.

I'm building an app that helps people discover local classes on a map
and book nearby.

We're inviting a small group of early teachers before launch so the map
already has some great classes available.

Your classes seemed like a great fit so I thought I'd reach out.

Would you be open to hosting your classes on the platform when we
launch?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Reply to Responses (10 minutes)

Common questions you may get: - How does the platform work? - What does
it cost? - Can I host private lessons or group classes? - How do
bookings work?

Keep responses simple and conversational.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Follow Up Previous Leads (10 minutes)

Follow up after **3--4 days**.

Example follow‑up:

Hi --- just following up in case my message got buried.

Would love to include your classes when we launch if you're interested.

Follow‑ups often double response rates.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Coding Time (50 minutes)

Return to development tasks after outreach.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Weekly Targets

Messages sent: **100--140**

Expected results: - 10--20 conversations - 5--10 teachers interested

After \~3 weeks you should have:

**20--30 teachers ready for launch.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Teacher Recruitment Strategy

## Focus on ONE city first

Start with:

**Dublin**

Then expand later to: - Cork - Galway

Marketplace success depends on **local density**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Recruit Category Clusters

Instead of random teachers, recruit clusters such as:

-   4 guitar teachers
-   4 art teachers
-   4 cooking teachers
-   3 photography teachers

Choice increases bookings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Launch Density Trick

Ask each teacher to schedule **2--3 sessions before launch**.

Example:

15 teachers × 3 sessions = **45 map sessions**

The map immediately looks active.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Do **consistent outreach every day**, even if it's just 15--20 messages.

Small daily effort compounds into a strong launch supply.
